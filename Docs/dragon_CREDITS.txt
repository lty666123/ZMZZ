Dragon - Fully Animated
========================================
Author: Cethiel
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/dragon-fully-animated
License: CC0

Source pack contains:
- Attack 1
- Attack 2
- Death
- Hurt
- Idle
- Idle Battle
- Walking

Unity sprite sheets were generated from the full source pack. Each frame is
362 x 222 (50% of the original 725 x 445):

- dragon_attack1.png: 41 frames, every 4th source frame, 8 x 6 cells
- dragon_attack2.png: 34 frames, every 6th source frame, 8 x 5 cells
- dragon_death.png: 31 frames, every 10th source frame, 8 x 4 cells
- dragon_hurt.png: 16 frames, every 4th source frame, 8 x 2 cells
- dragon_idle.png: 17 frames, every 10th source frame, 8 x 3 cells
- dragon_idle_battle.png: 14 frames, every 10th source frame, 8 x 2 cells
- dragon_walking.png: 17 frames, every 10th source frame, 8 x 3 cells
